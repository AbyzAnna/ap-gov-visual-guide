Civil Liberties and Civil Rights

How does the Constitution protect individuals from government (civil liberties) and protect groups from discrimination (civil rights)? Unit 3 is case-heavy: it contains 9 of the 15 required Supreme Court cases. Learn each case's facts, the clause involved, and its holding.
