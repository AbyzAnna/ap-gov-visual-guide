Foundations of American Democracy

Why did Americans create the government they did? Unit 1 follows the ideas (Enlightenment thought), the failures (Articles of Confederation), the compromises (Constitutional Convention), and the design features (separation of powers, checks and balances, federalism) that shape everything else in the course.
