Topic 3.7

Selective Incorporation

Essential QuestionWhat are the implications of the doctrine of selective incorporation?
In one minute

The Bill of Rights originally limited only the federal government. The Fourteenth Amendment (1868) says no state shall 'deprive any person of life, liberty, or property, without due process of law.' Through selective incorporation, the Supreme Court has applied most Bill of Rights protections to the states, case by case. McDonald v. Chicago (2010) incorporated the Second Amendment.

2Required Case: McDonald v. Chicago

REQUIRED CASE

McDonald v. Chicago (2010)

5–4
Second Amendment · Fourteenth Amendment due process clause
Facts
Chicago banned handgun possession by most private citizens. Otis McDonald, a retired Black custodian in a high-crime neighborhood, wanted a handgun at home for self-defense.
Constitutional question
Does the Second Amendment right recognized in Heller apply to states and cities through the Fourteenth Amendment?
Decision
Yes. Justice Alito: the right to keep and bear arms for self-defense is fundamental and is incorporated through the 14th Amendment's due process clause. (Justice Thomas concurred using the privileges or immunities clause.)
Why it matters
Extended Heller to every state and city; a classic example of selective incorporation limiting state power.
Memory hook: McDonald = Make it apply to Different governments (states & cities).
